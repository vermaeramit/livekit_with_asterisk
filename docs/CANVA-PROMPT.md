Using Canva, create a 1920 x 1080 presentation slide: a clean, modern process
flowchart showing how an AI voice agent handles a phone call, and what it can do
at each step.

STYLE
Light background, near white with a very subtle cool tint. Deep indigo as the
primary colour, a warm orange used only for decision shapes, teal only for the
start and end. Rounded rectangles with soft shadows, thin connecting arrows with
small arrowheads. A clean sans-serif throughout — bold for headings inside the
boxes, regular and one size smaller for the supporting line. Generous white
space. Corporate, calm, uncluttered. No photographs, no clip-art people, no
gradients.

TITLE, top left, dark indigo, bold:
How a call works

SUBTITLE, directly beneath the title, grey, regular weight:
From the moment your dialler connects it, to the report afterwards

MAIN FLOW — five boxes in a row across the middle of the slide, left to right,
joined by arrows. Each box has a bold heading and one smaller grey line beneath:

1  Solid teal pill, white text
   Call arrives
   handed over by your dialler

2  White box, indigo border
   Listens
   11 Indian languages · interrupt any time

3  Orange-outlined diamond, slightly larger
   Understands
   decides what it needs

4  White box, indigo border
   Speaks
   natural Indian voices

5  Solid teal pill, white text
   Call ends
   or goes to a colleague

BRANCHES — from box 3 "Understands", three arrows fan downward to three white
cards sitting in a row below the main flow, each with a small indigo line icon
in its top-left corner. All three rejoin the flow with an arrow going up into
box 4 "Speaks":

   icon: an open book
   Knowledge base
   answers from your own documents

   icon: two interlocking gears
   Your systems
   your APIs called mid-conversation

   icon: two people with an arrow between them
   Human handover
   transferred to a person on request

ABOVE THE FLOW — one small white card with a dashed arrow pointing down into
box 2 "Listens", icon of a pause symbol:
   Queue
   over the limit, callers wait and hear a message

BOTTOM STRIP — a full width light indigo band across the bottom of the slide,
with a bold white heading on the left and six short items in a row beside it,
each with a small white line icon above the word:
   Set per campaign, no deployment
   Prompt · Voice · Knowledge · Tools · Limits · Keys

FOOTER, bottom right, small grey text:
Recording, transcript and timings kept for every call

Use exactly the words above and no others. Keep every line short enough to sit
on one line inside its box.
